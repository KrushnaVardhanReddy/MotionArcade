# Sustainability & Revenue Model

MotionArcade operates on a strict ethical principle: **Education and movement for children must always be 100% free.**

No child, parent, or school will ever encounter a paywall, locked content pack, or subscription to access learning games. The software is open-source, fully offline-capable, and completely free for home and educational use.

To sustainably fund continuous development, pose-model optimization, and community content curation, revenue is generated through two distinct channels: **Adult Fitness** and **Plug-and-Play Living Room Hardware**.

---

## The Non-Negotiable Core: 100% Free for Kids & Schools

| Target User | Cost | What They Receive |
|---|---|---|
| **Kids at Home** | **$0 (Free Forever)** | All games, all learning packs, progress tracking, offline play, zero ads, zero in-app purchases. |
| **Teachers & Schools** | **$0 (Free Forever)** | Browser/WASM app, offline USB installer, roster management, classroom turn rotation, progress reports, and curriculum packs. |

No educational content is ever gated. Anyone with a laptop, Chromebook, or webcam can use the full platform without spending a dime.

### Stream 1: Adult Fitness, HIIT & Skill Coaching (Software Subscription)

While educational games are free for children, adults who want to utilize the motion engine for personal health and home training subscribe to a dedicated **Fitness Pass**.

- **Pricing:** ~$6.99 – $8.99 / month or $49 – $59 / year.
- **What Adults Pay For:**
  - Structured workout programs (HIIT cardio, core, mobility, active recovery).
  - Accurate rep counting, pace guidance, and coarse form cues ("squat deeper", "chest up").
  - Extended workout interval timers and progress metrics.
  - Asymmetric Family Challenges (parent gets workout reps while child gets math/spelling targets).
- **The Philosophy:** Adults regularly pay for gym memberships, fitness apps, and home workout tools. Adult subscriptions directly sponsor free, ad-free learning for millions of children globally.

---

### Stream 2: Turnkey Living Room TV Appliance (Hardware Convenience)

The software runs free on any laptop or Chromebook. However, non-technical parents frequently encounter friction connecting laptops to TVs via HDMI cables and setting up webcams.

To solve this, MotionArcade offers an optional, dedicated **Plug-and-Play Living Room Console** (a direct, accessible alternative to the $200+ Nex Playground):

- **What It Is:** An ultra-compact mini PC (e.g., Intel N100) pre-installed with MotionArcade in auto-boot Kiosk mode, bundled with an optimized wide-angle 1080p webcam and TV mount.
- **Retail Price:** **$149 – $179** (one-time purchase).
- **Bill of Materials (BOM) Cost:** ~$100 – $115 per unit.
- **Unit Margin:** **$40 – $65 net profit per box**.
- **Value Proposition:** Parents do not pay for software—they pay for zero-friction convenience: plug one HDMI cable into the TV, power it on, and their kids are playing with gesture menus instantly.

## Ethical Summary

1. **No predatory mechanics:** No microtransactions, no loot boxes, no tracking of minors' personal data.
2. **No educational paywalls:** Every child and school has equal access to every lesson.
3. **Adults fund children:** Healthy adult habits fund active child education.
4. **Convenience is commercial; software is free:** Anyone can build or run it themselves for free; customers pay only if they want a ready-to-use living-room TV box.
